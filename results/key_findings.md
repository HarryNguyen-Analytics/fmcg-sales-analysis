# Key Findings

## 1. Sales Performance
- Total revenue reached approximately $19.95M from 3.80M units sold.
- Revenue increased from $3.17M in 2022 to $8.44M in 2023.
- Revenue remained stable in 2024 at $8.35M, decreasing only 1.05% from 2023.

## 2. Product Performance
- Yogurt was the largest revenue category, generating approximately $8.23M and contributing 41.23% of total revenue.
- Yogurt and Milk together contributed approximately 61.76% of total revenue.
- YO-029 was the highest-revenue SKU at approximately $931.9K, followed by YO-005 at $913.4K and YO-012 at $899.4K.
- The product portfolio expanded from 20 SKUs in 2022 to 30 SKUs in 2023 and remained at 30 SKUs in 2024.
- Among the 10 SKUs first observed in 2023, YO-020 generated the highest cumulative revenue at approximately $636.6K.

## 3. Channel & Regional Performance
- Revenue was almost evenly split across channels: Retail 33.36%, E-commerce 33.35%, and Discount 33.29%.
- Retail generated the highest channel revenue at approximately $6.66M, while E-commerce recorded the highest unit sales at 1.27M units.
- Regional revenue was also highly balanced: PL-South 33.41%, PL-North 33.40%, and PL-Central 33.19%.
- Retail ranked first by revenue in PL-Central and PL-North, while E-commerce ranked first in PL-South.

## 4. Promotion Performance
- Promotion represented only 14.92% of sales records but generated approximately 25.57% of total revenue.
- Average units sold increased from 17.44 without promotion to 34.06 under promotion.
- Average revenue increased from $91.50 without promotion to $179.24 under promotion.
- SnackBar showed the largest relative increase in average units sold under promotion at 97.18%.
- Yogurt recorded the highest absolute promoted performance at 36.64 average units sold and $192.05 average revenue.

## 5. Inventory & Delivery
- Average stock availability was almost identical across categories at approximately 158 units.
- Milk had the highest stock-to-sales ratio at 9.0, followed by Juice at 8.8, while Yogurt had the lowest ratio at 7.3.
- This suggests Milk and Juice carry relatively more stock compared with their sales levels, while Yogurt uses a similar stock level against stronger sales.
- Average delivery times were highly consistent across categories, ranging only from 3.00 to 3.03 days.
- After excluding three anomalous negative-value records, no remaining records showed units_sold exceeding stock_available. (UPDATED)
